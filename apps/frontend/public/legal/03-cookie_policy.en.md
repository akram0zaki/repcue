# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** [Insert Date]  
This Cookie Policy explains how **RepCue B.V.** ("RepCue", "we") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and within our App.

## 1. What Are Cookies and SDKs?
Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites. Our App may also use SDKs (software development kits) that function similarly to cookies for analytics and performance.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use
- **Strictly Necessary:** required for core functions (security, login).  
- **Functional:** remember preferences, settings, language.  
- **Analytics/Performance:** help us understand usage and improve services.  
- **Advertising/Marketing (consent‑based):** personalize and measure ads (if used).

## 3. Examples & Retention (Illustrative)
- **GA4:** _ga, _gid (analytics) – 14–26 months (per Google defaults).  
- **Firebase Analytics:** app usage metrics – retention configurable.  
- **Consent Cookie:** stores your cookie choices – 6–12 months.  
A maintained cookie list appears in "Cookie Settings".

## 4. Your Choices
On first visit, we display a **cookie banner** allowing you to **Accept All**, **Reject Non‑Essential**, or **Manage Preferences**. You can change your choices anytime via "Cookie Settings". Essential cookies cannot be disabled.

## 5. Third‑Party Cookies
Some cookies are set by third parties (e.g., analytics providers). Their privacy policies apply to their cookies.

## 6. Do Not Track
We currently do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals due to the lack of standardization.

## 7. Updates
We may update this Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
