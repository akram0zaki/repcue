## 🎯 How to Get Started

### Step 1: Complete Your First Workout
Just use RepCue as you normally would! Your AI Coach starts learning from day one.

### Step 2: Visit the Coach Tab
After a few workouts, tap the **Coach** icon in the bottom navigation to see your first insights.

### Step 3: Customize Your Experience
Go to **Settings → AI Coach** to:
- Choose your coach's personality (Zen, Energy, or Logic)
- Enable/disable AI-powered insights
- Control celebration animations and sounds
- Set post-workout survey frequency
- Toggle insights on home screen

### Step 4: Stay Consistent
The more you train, the smarter your Coach becomes! Aim for:
- ✅ At least 3 workouts per week for basic insights
- ✅ 7+ days of history for AI-powered insights
- ✅ Provide post-workout feedback when possible

## 🔒 Privacy & Data

### Your Data Stays Private
- **Offline-First**: All core features work without internet
- **Local Storage**: Your workout history lives on your device in IndexedDB
- **Optional Sync**: Sign in to sync across devices (end-to-end encrypted)
- **AI is Optional**: AI insights require internet, but you can disable them anytime

### AI Insights
- Only enabled if you opt-in
- Data sent to AI is anonymized (no personal identifiers)
- AI responses cached for 24 hours to minimize data transmission
- You control when AI runs (manual refresh available)

### No Third-Party Tracking
- RepCue does not share data with advertisers
- No analytics tracking without consent
- Self-hosted deployment option available for ultimate privacy

**Bottom line**: Your fitness journey is yours. We just help you track it!

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Coach Page Not Showing Insights?
**Solution**: Complete at least 3-5 workouts over several days. The Coach needs data to generate insights!

### Want More/Fewer Insights?
**Solution**: Go to **Settings → AI Coach → Insight Frequency** and adjust the slider.

### Confetti Too Much?
**Solution**: Turn on **Reduce Motion** in your device's accessibility settings, or disable celebration animations in RepCue settings.

### AI Insights Not Working?
**Solution**:
1. Check you have internet connection
2. Verify AI Insights are enabled in Settings
3. Wait 24 hours if you recently checked (results are cached)
4. Try "Force Refresh" button on Coach page

### Changed Persona but Messages Look the Same?
**Solution**: Dismiss old insights and refresh the Coach page. New insights will use your selected persona's tone.
