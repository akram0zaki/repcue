# Community Guidelines

RepCue aims to foster a supportive, respectful community. By participating in community features (if enabled), you agree to follow these rules.

## 1. Respect & Inclusion
Treat others with kindness and respect. Do not harass, bully, demean, or discriminate against individuals or groups.

## 2. Prohibited Content & Behavior
- Hate speech or threats of violence  
- Sexual or explicit content involving minors; non-consensual content  
- Glorification of self‑harm or dangerous acts  
- Medical misinformation presented as fact  
- Spam, scams, or commercial solicitations without authorization  
- Posting others’ personal data without consent

## 3. Intellectual Property
Share only content you created or have the right to share. Respect copyrights and trademarks. Comply with takedown requests.

## 4. Safety & Privacy
Do not share sensitive personal or financial information. Report abuse via in‑app tools or **[support@email]**.

## 5. Moderation & Enforcement
We may remove content or suspend/terminate accounts for violations. Repeated or severe violations may result in permanent bans.

## 6. Reporting & Appeals
Use reporting tools or email **[support@email]**. You may appeal moderation actions via **[appeals@email]**.

## 7. Local Norms
Follow applicable laws and respect cultural norms in your country of residence.
