## 1. Scope
This Policy applies to users of the RepCue mobile app and website worldwide, including the EU, Egypt, and MENA. We apply GDPR standards globally and acknowledge that **mandatory local laws** may grant additional rights.

## 2. Data We Collect
- **Identification & Contact:** name, email, account IDs, language.  
- **Profile & Preferences:** age range, goals, time/location preferences, interface language.  
- **Usage & Device:** IP address, device identifiers, OS/app version, crash logs, performance and interaction events.  
- **Subscription & Billing:** plan tier, status, renewal dates, transactions (processed by app stores/payment providers).  
- **Communications:** support requests, feedback, survey responses.  
We **do not** seek special‑category health data; avoid entering sensitive medical information.

## 3. Purposes & Legal Bases
- **Provide & personalize services** (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – contract).  
- **Analytics, safety, and improvement** (Art. 6(1)(f) – legitimate interests).  
- **Marketing communications** (Art. 6(1)(a) – consent; opt out any time).  
- **Legal compliance** (Art. 6(1)(c) – tax, accounting, regulatory).

## 4. Cookies & Similar Technologies
Our website uses cookies/SDKs for necessary operations, analytics, and (with consent) marketing. See the **Cookie Policy** for details and choices.

## 5. Retention
We keep personal data only as long as necessary:
- Account data: life of the account; delete or anonymize within **24 months** of closure.  
- Billing/transaction data: up to **7 years** to comply with tax law.  
- Analytics logs: typically **14–26 months** (per provider settings).  
We may retain records longer where required by law or to establish/defend legal claims.

## 6. Sharing & Recipients
We share data with **processors** that help us operate the App, including:  
hosting/CDN, analytics, error logging, payment processing, customer support, and content tooling. We sign **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)** requiring security and confidentiality, and we do not permit processors to use your data for their own purposes.

## 7. International Transfers
Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA (e.g., to Egypt or other MENA countries), we implement appropriate safeguards such as **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** and risk‑mitigating technical measures. We minimize the personal data transferred and regularly review vendor practices.

## 8. Your Rights
You have rights to **access**, **rectify**, **erase**, **restrict**, **object**, and **data portability**; and to **withdraw consent** at any time without affecting prior processing. To exercise rights, contact **[privacy@email]**.  
You may lodge a complaint with the **Dutch Supervisory Authority** (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or your local authority.

## 9. Security
We use technical and organizational measures to protect data, including encryption in transit, access controls, backups, monitoring, vendor due diligence, and incident response procedures. No system is 100% secure; report incidents to **[security@email]**.

## 10. Children
We do not knowingly collect data from children under **16** without verifiable parental consent (or higher national threshold). If you believe a minor provided data without consent, contact us to delete it.

## 11. Local Compliance Notes
We apply GDPR globally but do not limit **mandatory local consumer or privacy rights** (e.g., Egypt/MENA). This Policy will be adapted if we later enter jurisdictions with additional requirements (e.g., CCPA in California).
