**Parties:**  
- **Controller:** RepCue B.V., [Company Address], KvK [KvK Number] (“Controller”)  
- **Processor:** [Processor Legal Name], [Address] (“Processor”)

**Effective date:** [Insert Date] • **Duration:** For the term of the underlying services agreement.

---

## 1. Subject Matter & Purpose
Processor will process Personal Data on behalf of Controller to provide hosting, storage, analytics, payments, communications, content processing, and related services (the “Services”).

## 2. Nature & Categories of Data; Data Subjects
- **Data Subjects:** users of the App and website; subscribers; visitors.  
- **Categories:** identification and contact data; profile/preferences; usage/device; subscription/billing; communications.  
- **Special Categories:** not intended. Processor shall not intentionally process special categories without Controller’s prior written authorization.

## 3. Processor Obligations
Processor shall:  
(a) process Personal Data only on documented instructions of Controller;  
(b) ensure confidentiality;  
(c) implement appropriate **technical and organizational measures (TOMs)**;  
(d) assist Controller with data subject requests and privacy impact assessments;  
(e) notify Controller **without undue delay** and within **72 hours** where feasible after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach;  
(f) delete or return all Personal Data at termination, unless law requires retention; and  
(g) make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance.

## 4. Sub‑Processors
Processor may engage sub‑processors with Controller’s **general written authorization**, provided Processor imposes data protection obligations no less protective than this DPA. Processor will maintain an up‑to‑date list of sub‑processors and notify Controller of changes, allowing objections on reasonable grounds.

## 5. International Transfers
Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside the EEA without appropriate safeguards (e.g., **SCCs**) and shall conduct transfer risk assessments and implement supplementary measures where necessary.

## 6. Security
Processor shall implement TOMs proportionate to the risks, including (as applicable): encryption in transit, logical access controls, least‑privilege, secure development practices, vulnerability management, logging/monitoring, backups and recovery, incident response, and personnel training.

## 7. Audits
Upon reasonable notice, Processor shall allow audits by Controller or a mutually agreed independent auditor, subject to confidentiality and security requirements.

## 8. Liability & Indemnity
The parties’ liability is as set out in the underlying agreement. Processor shall indemnify Controller for losses caused by Processor’s breach of this DPA, subject to agreed limitations.

## 9. Termination & Deletion
Upon termination of the Services, Processor shall delete or return all Personal Data (at Controller’s choice) and delete existing copies unless law requires storage.
